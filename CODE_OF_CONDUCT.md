# Contributor Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participation in the Deslopify project and its community. Please read and follow these guidelines to make Deslopify a welcoming and positive environment for all contributors.

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and accessible environment for all contributors, regardless of:

- Gender, gender identity, gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability, physical or mental ability
- Socioeconomic status
- Religion or spiritual beliefs
- Age
- Ethnicity or race

We are committed to being inclusive and creating an open community where people feel comfortable and safe to contribute.

## Expected Behavior

In all project spaces, participants should:

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness towards other people
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- Ask questions when unsure, offer help to others
- Treat everyone equally regardless of their background or status
- Focus on what is best for the community and users

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or harassing comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Insisting on gender pronouns
- Physical violence, threats, or intimidation
- Taking up excessive time in discussions or meetings
- Deliberately excluding someone from the community
- Sharing personal information of others without consent

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, documentation, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement will be carried out fairly and consistently, based on the following graduated approach:

1. **First offense**: Warning and education on appropriate behavior
2. **Second offense**: Temporary ban from the project
3. **Third offense**: Permanent ban from the project

## How to Report

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior:

1. Report it immediately to the project maintainers
2. Provide clear, specific details about the incident
3. Your identity will be kept confidential when reporting
4. All reports will be taken seriously and investigated

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces including:

- GitHub repository
- Discord servers (if any)
- Social media platforms
- Conferences and events
- Online forums
- Code reviews and discussions
